# Solat 1.1.0

> **In development.** This file is filled in as each phase of the [roadmap](../ROADMAP.md) is merged.

Solat 1.1.0 makes prayer notifications reliable.

## Installing

1. Download the APK below and open it on your phone. Android may ask you to allow installing apps from your browser or file manager.
2. If you already have Solat 1.0.1, this version installs over it and keeps your zone and settings.

Requires Android 10 or newer.

## What's changed

**Prayer notifications**
- Switching a prayer's notification off now stops it straight away. Before, the next one still came.
- The countdown and notifications follow Malaysia time even when your phone is set to another time zone. Before, they could be hours out.
- On the last day of a month, the next month's prayer times are downloaded, so the next morning's notifications aren't missed.
- After each notification, the next ones are set straight away, instead of waiting for the once-a-day background check.
- Notifications are set again after your phone restarts, after Solat is updated, after the clock is changed, and when you allow exact alarms. Low battery no longer delays them.
- After midnight, the prayer screen moves on to the new day by itself.
